# Country Rules

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. General provisions

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and gathers the particulars of each country in which the Platform operates. The rules applicable to a Booking are those of the **country where the Space is located**, without prejudice to the mandatory consumer protection rules of the Renter's country of residence.

1.2. **Reference rates.** The rates indicated refer to the tax levied on the Platform's Service Fee, and are **reference values** for configuration purposes. The actual taxation may vary according to municipality, state, province, the taxpayer's regime, and legislative changes, and will be the rate shown on the receipt.

1.3. **Precedence of the more protective local rule.** Where local law grants the consumer, the User, or the data subject broader rights than those set out in the Platform's documents, local law prevails.

1.4. **Professional registration.** The bodies indicated are reference points for the verification required in Listings marked "requires professional registration." Other competent bodies may be accepted depending on the activity.

1.5. **Right of withdrawal.** The period indicated is automatically applied to consumers before the start of the first Occurrence (Cancellation and Refund Policy). "Not configured" means there is no automatic period, without prejudice to local legal rights.

1.6. **Payment methods.** "Card" includes Visa, Mastercard and American Express, subject to availability. Methods marked with an asterisk (\*) do not allow Security Deposit pre-authorization (Guarantor and Security Deposit document).
