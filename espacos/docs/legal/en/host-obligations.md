# Host Obligations

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and establishes the Host's obligations when listing and making Spaces available on the Platform. Non-compliance subjects the Host to the measures in Penalties and Measures, including suspension of the Listing and of the account.

## 2. Right to grant use of the Space

2.1. The Host represents and warrants that they have the **right to grant use of the Space** to third parties by the hour, whether as owner, tenant, borrower (*comodatário*), or holder of another right, with sufficient authorization to do so.

2.2. Where the Host is not the owner, they must ensure that their contract (for example, a lease) **does not prohibit** temporary assignment, partial subletting, or sharing, or must obtain the owner's **written authorization**. They must also verify that the **condominium bylaws or regulations** and the building's rules permit the activity and the flow of people resulting from the Bookings.

2.3. The Platform may request proof (title, contract, authorization) at any time and suspend the Listing until it is provided.

## 3. Licenses and regulatory compliance

3.1. The Host must keep valid all licenses required for the Space and for the activities permitted there, such as:
- (a) **Brazil**: operating permit (*alvará de funcionamento*), health authority (*Vigilância Sanitária*) license for health-care establishments, a technical director when required, AVCB/CLCB fire department certificate, radiology license when X-ray equipment is present, health-care service waste management plan (PGRSS);
- (b) **Portugal**: licensing by the ERS — Entidade Reguladora da Saúde (Health Regulatory Authority);
- (c) **Spain**: regional health authorization (RD 1277/2003);
- (d) **Italy**: regional *autorizzazione sanitaria*;
- (e) **France**: ERP (*établissement recevant du public*) compliance and accessibility;
- (f) **United Kingdom**: registration with the **CQC** (Care Quality Commission, England) for regulated activities;
- (g) **Mexico**: **COFEPRIS** notice/license for health-care establishments;
- (h) **Japan**: 診療所開設届 (clinic establishment notification) for clinics;
- (i) **United Arab Emirates**: *trade licence* and DHA/DoH/MOHAP licenses;
- (j) other countries: equivalent licenses indicated in Country Rules and in local legislation.

3.2. If the Space is a health-care Space and the health license is tied to a technical director or to specific activities, the Host must restrict the activities permitted in the Listing to the licensed scope and disclose whether the Renter needs their own registration with the health authority.

3.3. The Host must immediately notify the Platform and deactivate the Listing in case of closure, suspension, or expiration of a license.

## 4. Insurance

4.1. It is strongly recommended that the Host maintain **property insurance** (fire, electrical damage, equipment) and **civil liability insurance** covering use by third parties. Where the law or the condominium requires insurance, obtaining it is mandatory.

4.2. The Host must check whether their policy permits the shared use of the Space. The Security Deposit and the Guarantor do not replace insurance.

## 5. Accurate Listing

5.1. The Listing must accurately describe: the category; address and neighborhood; maximum capacity; area; equipment (brand, model and condition, where relevant); amenities; accessibility; cameras in common areas; permitted and prohibited activities; internal rules; condominium or building rules; cleaning buffer; cancellation policy; Guarantor policy; the Security Deposit amount; and whether professional registration is required.

5.2. Photos must be current and of the actual Space. It is prohibited to use merely illustrative images without labeling them as such, to omit material defects, or to advertise unavailable equipment.

5.3. **Prices** must be stated in the currency of the Space's country. The **Cleaning Fee** may not exceed 30% of the Base Amount, and the **Security Deposit** may not exceed 3 times the Base Amount.

5.4. An inaccurate Listing subjects the Host to a refund to the Renter and a Warning (Penalties and Measures).

## 6. Actual availability

6.1. The Host must list only genuinely idle time slots and keep the calendar up to date, blocking unavailable dates. The calendar must reflect other uses of the Space, including the Host's own use and use through other platforms.

6.2. Available time slots must comply with the Platform's limits (6:00 a.m. to 11:00 p.m., no overnight stay).

## 7. Equipment, maintenance and calibration

7.1. The Host must keep the advertised equipment in working order, with the **preventive and corrective maintenance**, **calibration**, **validation** and **testing** required by legislation and the manufacturer (for example, biological indicator testing of autoclaves, dosimetry and radiometric surveys for X-ray equipment, maintenance of compressors and suction units), keeping the records.

7.2. Non-functioning equipment must be removed from the Listing or flagged before the Booking. If a failure occurs after confirmation, the Host must notify the Renter immediately, who may cancel the affected Occurrences at no cost.

## 8. Cleaning between Bookings

8.1. The Host must deliver the Space **clean and sanitized** at the start of each Occurrence, observing the category's health protocols, and use the configured cleaning buffer for this purpose.

8.2. At health-care Spaces, the Host must provide appropriate containers for infectious and sharps waste and maintain a health-care service waste collection contract, when required.

## 9. Access on time

9.1. The Host must ensure the **Renter's access at the start time** of the Occurrence, with clear instructions (keys, codes, reception, visitor registration), and must keep a contact available during the Occurrence for emergencies.

9.2. Lack of access results in a full refund to the Renter and a Warning to the Host.

## 10. Privacy of the Host's own files

10.1. The Host must **remove or lock away** records, files, documents, computers, devices, and personal property not included in the Listing, in particular data of their own patients and clients, complying with their duties of professional confidentiality and data protection.

10.2. It is prohibited to install cameras or recording devices in treatment areas, offices, therapy rooms, bathrooms and changing rooms. Cameras in common areas must be disclosed in the Listing and clearly signposted on site.

## 11. Taxes on income

11.1. The Host is solely responsible for **declaring and paying the taxes** on the amounts received, for issuing the tax documents required by their legislation, and for complying with ancillary obligations.

11.2. The Platform may be required to collect the Host's tax data, withhold taxes, or report income to authorities, for example:
- (a) **European Union**: **DAC7** Directive — annual reporting of sellers' income to tax authorities;
- (b) **United States**: **Form 1099-K**;
- (c) **Mexico**: VAT/income tax withholding by digital platforms (Ley del IVA, Chapter III Bis);
- (d) **India**: TCS under the GST, where applicable;
- (e) **Brazil**: applicable reporting and withholding obligations, including during the transition to CBS/IBS (LC 214/2025).

11.3. The Host must keep their tax data (for example, CPF/CNPJ, NIF, EIN, RFC) and banking details up to date. Missing this data may result in payouts being withheld until it is corrected.

## 12. Non-discrimination

12.1. The Host may not refuse, cancel, or impose different conditions on Bookings based on race, color, ethnicity, origin, nationality, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, marital status, or other protected characteristic. Refusals must be based on objective and legitimate criteria (availability, compatibility of the activity, professional registration, Platform history).

## 13. Communication and responses

13.1. The Host must respond to messages and Booking requests within **24 hours**. Requests not answered within this period automatically expire, which may affect the Listing's position in search results.

13.2. All communication relating to Bookings must take place through the Platform. Requesting payment or contact outside the Platform for the purpose of circumventing it is prohibited.

## 14. Cancellations by the Host

14.1. The Host must honor confirmed Bookings. Cancellations result in a full refund to the Renter, a penalty of 0% (with 168 hours or more notice), 10% (from 48 to less than 168 hours), or 25% (less than 48 hours) of the Base Amount of the cancelled Occurrences, and a Warning. **3 cancellations in 12 months** lead to suspension of the Host account, except for proven mitigating circumstances (Cancellation and Refund Policy).

## 15. Relationship with Renters

15.1. The Host must respect the Renter's professional autonomy, not interfere with appointments, and not approach the Renter's End Clients to offer their own services based on information obtained through the Booking.

15.2. The Host is not technically responsible for the Renter's acts, but must report to the Platform and, where applicable, to the authorities, any situations posing a risk to health or safety that they witness.

## 16. Service Fee and payout

16.1. From the amount paid by the Renter (Base Amount plus Cleaning Fee), the Host's Service Fee of **5%** is deducted. The payout is released **24 hours after the start** of the first Occurrence, as set out in the International Payments document.
