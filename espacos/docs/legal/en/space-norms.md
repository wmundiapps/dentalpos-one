# Conduct and Respect for Spaces Rules

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and establishes the conduct rules that Renters, their assistants, End Clients and visitors must observe at the Spaces, as well as duties of mutual respect between Hosts and Renters.

1.2. The following apply cumulatively: (a) these rules; (b) the **Space's internal rules** published by the Host in the Listing; (c) the **condominium or building rules** stated in the Listing; (d) applicable legislation and professional and health rules. The Renter declares that they know and accept them upon confirming the Booking.

1.3. Non-compliance subjects the offender to the measures set out in Penalties and Measures.

## 2. General principles

2.1. The Space is another person's workplace. The Renter must use it with the same care they would give their own establishment, leaving it **in the condition in which it was received**, ready for the next professional.

2.2. The Booking's times, the capacity, the declared activity, and the Host's instructions must be respected.

## 3. Order, cleanliness and upkeep

3.1. The Renter must: keep the Space organized; not move fixed furniture or equipment without authorization; put items back in their places; remove their own trash or dispose of it in the designated bins; and immediately report any damage, malfunction or accident.

3.2. It is prohibited to: glue, nail or affix objects to the walls; change network, climate-control or equipment settings beyond normal use; remove any property of the Host from the Space.

3.3. **Check-in and check-out photos.** It is strongly recommended that the Renter record, through the Platform, time-stamped photos upon arrival and departure. These photos are the primary evidence in case of a dispute about the condition of the Space.

## 4. Noise and sound

4.1. The Renter must respect the building's noise limits and municipal legislation, as well as quiet hours. Sound equipment, music and microphones may only be used if provided for in the Listing or authorized by the Host, at a compatible volume.

4.2. In health-care and psychology Spaces, silence and discretion in common areas are essential to the privacy of other professionals' patients.

## 5. Condominium or building rules

5.1. The Renter must follow the building's rules on reception/security, identification, use of elevators, parking, operating hours, movement through common areas, and visitor registration.

5.2. Fines applied by the condominium or building management due to the conduct of the Renter, their assistants or End Clients are passed on to the Renter, in accordance with Penalties and Measures.

## 6. Biosafety at health-care Spaces

6.1. At Spaces in the dentistry, medicine, psychology, physiotherapy, aesthetics, nutrition and veterinary categories, the Renter is the **technically responsible party for their own acts** and for the procedures they perform, and must comply with the health and professional-board rules, in particular:
- (a) **Personal protective equipment (PPE)**: use and provide to their assistants the PPE required for each procedure;
- (b) **Sterilization**: use their own properly sterilized instruments, or use the Space's autoclave only if authorized and in accordance with the Host's protocol, recording cycles when required;
- (c) **Disinfection**: disinfect surfaces, chairs, examination tables and equipment after each appointment and at the end of the Occurrence, in accordance with the health protocol;
- (d) **Health-care service waste**: segregate and dispose of infectious, sharps, chemical and common waste in the appropriate containers provided by the Host, in accordance with local health regulations (in Brazil, ANVISA RDC No. 222/2018 and related rules), and it is prohibited to leave waste outside the collection bins or to dispose of it with common waste;
- (e) **Medications and controlled materials**: not to be stored at the Space, and specific legislation must be observed;
- (f) **Radiation**: operate X-ray equipment only with proper qualification and in accordance with radiological protection rules.

6.2. The Host must provide the advertised structural conditions (waste bins, sink, autoclave, when advertised), but is not liable for the Renter's technical acts.

6.3. Incidents involving biological risk (sharps accident, spill, exposure) must be reported immediately to the Host and handled in accordance with the health protocol.

## 7. Professional confidentiality and privacy

7.1. The Renter must preserve **professional confidentiality** and the privacy of their patients and clients, conducting appointments so that they cannot be heard or seen by third parties, and not leaving documents, medical records, prescriptions, exam results or devices containing personal data at the Space.

7.2. It is **prohibited to access, consult, copy or photograph records, files, documents, computers or systems belonging to the Host** or to other professionals who use the Space, even if accessible. Cabinets, drawers and computers not indicated as available in the Listing may not be opened or used.

7.3. A Renter who uses the Space's computer or network must log out of their sessions and must not save patient or client data on the Host's equipment.

7.4. The Renter is the data controller for the personal data of their End Clients and is liable for its processing under applicable data protection legislation.

## 8. Use of equipment

8.1. The equipment described in the Listing may only be operated by a person who is **legally qualified and technically trained**, and in accordance with the manufacturer's and the Host's instructions.

8.2. Upon detecting a malfunction, the Renter must stop using the equipment and notify the Host. Attempting repairs, opening equipment, or altering calibrations is prohibited.

8.3. Consumable supplies may only be used if the Listing indicates that they are included.

## 9. Respect and non-discrimination

9.1. Hosts and Renters must treat each other, as well as End Clients, building staff and other persons, with respect and courtesy.

9.2. Any form of discrimination based on race, color, ethnicity, origin, nationality, religion, disability, sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, age, marital status, pregnancy or other characteristic protected by law is prohibited, whether in accepting Bookings, serving End Clients, or interacting at the Space.

9.3. Moral or sexual harassment, intimidation, threats or violence are prohibited. Violation is a serious breach and leads to exclusion.

## 10. Safety

10.1. The Renter must know the escape routes, the location of fire extinguishers, and the emergency instructions provided by the Host; must not block exits; must not overload electrical installations; and must not use equipment that creates a fire risk without authorization.

10.2. It is prohibited to carry weapons, except in cases authorized by law and the building's rules; to use or store flammable, explosive or unlawful substances; and to smoke (including electronic cigarettes) anywhere in the Space, except in an expressly designated area.

10.3. The Renter must lock the Space upon leaving, in accordance with the instructions, and must not share access codes.

## 11. Accessibility

11.1. The Host must accurately describe the accessibility conditions (ramps, elevator, accessible bathroom, door widths). The Renter must verify these conditions before booking when serving persons with disabilities or reduced mobility.

11.2. Blocking accessible routes or reserved spaces is prohibited. Refusing to serve a person with a disability because of that disability is discrimination.

## 12. Animals

12.1. Animals are permitted only at Spaces in the veterinary category, when authorized by the Listing, or in the case of **guide dogs and service animals**, whose presence is guaranteed under applicable law.

## 13. Food and drink

13.1. Consumption of food and beverages must be limited to the areas designated by the Host. In health-care procedure rooms and laboratories, consumption is prohibited. Professional kitchens follow the health regulations for food handling.

## 14. Visitors and children

14.1. Only persons included in the number declared in the Booking may enter the Space. Visitors who are not part of the activity are not permitted.

14.2. Children and adolescents may be present only as End Clients, students or participants in the declared activity, or as companions of End Clients, under the responsibility of the responsible adult and the Renter, in compliance with child-protection rules.

## 15. Emergencies

15.1. In a medical emergency, fire or other risk, the priority is the safety of persons: call local emergency services (in Brazil, SAMU 192, Fire Department 193, Police 190), evacuate if necessary, and then notify the Host and the Platform.

15.2. Renters at health-care Spaces must be prepared to provide basic life support within the limits of their qualification.

## 16. Host's duties toward the Renter

16.1. The Host must respect the privacy of the Renter and their End Clients during the Occurrence, not entering the Space without prior notice, except in an emergency, and not interfering with appointments.

16.2. The Host may not install cameras in treatment areas, offices, therapy rooms, bathrooms or changing rooms. Cameras in common areas must be disclosed in the Listing and clearly signposted.
