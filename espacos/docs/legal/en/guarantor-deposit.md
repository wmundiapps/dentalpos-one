# Guarantor and Security Deposit

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and governs the two guarantees that may accompany a Booking: the **Security Deposit** (a pre-authorization on the Renter's payment method) and the **Guarantor** (a person who guarantees the Renter's financial obligations).

1.2. The guarantees are intended exclusively to cover amounts owed by the Renter in connection with the Booking: damage, extra cleaning, late departure, condominium fines, and penalties decided under Penalties and Measures, as well as any unpaid balance of the price.

## 2. Security Deposit

2.1. **Definition.** The Security Deposit is a **pre-authorization** (temporary hold on the credit limit) on the Renter's card or payment method that permits this operation. The amount is not charged unless there is an Incident decided against the Renter.

2.2. **Amount.** The amount of the Security Deposit is set by the Host in the Listing and **may not exceed 3 times the Base Amount** of the Booking. Where the amount set is higher than this limit, the limit prevails.

2.3. **Timing.** The pre-authorization is performed at Booking confirmation or before the start of the first Occurrence, depending on the validity period allowed by the issuer, and may be renewed for Bookings with several Occurrences.

2.4. **Release.** The Security Deposit is **released 72 hours after check-out** of the last Occurrence if no Incident is open. If an Incident is opened within that period, the portion corresponding to the amount claimed remains held until the decision, and the remainder is released.

2.5. **Capture.** Only amounts acknowledged by the Renter or decided by the Platform under the Resolution and Disputes Center may be captured. The Renter receives a breakdown of the amount captured.

2.6. **Issuer timeframe.** After release by the Platform, unblocking the credit limit may take a few days, depending on the issuer.

## 3. Payment methods without pre-authorization

3.1. The payment methods **Pix, boleto, OXXO, SPEI, Multibanco, konbini, bank transfer, BPAY, PSE, net banking, UPI, Bizum, MB WAY, Bit, PayID and Yape** do not allow pre-authorization.

3.2. When the Listing requires a Security Deposit and the Renter pays using one of these methods, the Security Deposit must be replaced, at the Renter's choice, by: (a) a **guarantee card** registered solely for the Security Deposit pre-authorization; or (b) a **Guarantor** accepted under this document.

## 4. Guarantor — requirement scenarios

4.1. The Host sets one of the following policies in the Listing:

| Policy | Effect |
|---|---|
| **None** (`none`) | No Guarantor is requested. |
| **Optional** (`optional`) | The Renter may name a Guarantor to give the Host more assurance, which may favor acceptance of request-based Bookings. |
| **Required** (`required`) | Every Booking requires an accepted Guarantor. |
| **Required above an amount** (`required_over_amount`) | A Guarantor is required when the total of the Booking is equal to or greater than the threshold amount set by the Host. |

4.2. The Guarantor requirement may not be used as a tool for discrimination. It must be applied uniformly to all Renters of that Listing.

## 5. Guarantor — details, acceptance and deadline

5.1. The Renter provides: full name, email, phone number (optional), identification document number, and relationship to the Renter (optional). The Renter represents that they have the Guarantor's authorization to share this data with the Platform for this purpose.

5.2. The Guarantor receives an invitation via an **individual, secure link** sent to their email, containing: identification of the Renter, the Space and the Occurrences; the total amount of the Booking; the limit of liability; and the full text of this document.

5.3. The Guarantor has **48 hours** to accept or decline. While there is no response, the Booking has the status "awaiting guarantor" and the payment is only authorized. A decline or lack of response within the deadline ends the request and releases the authorization in full, at no cost.

5.4. The Guarantor may **decline freely**, without justification and without any consequence to themselves.

5.5. The Guarantor must be **at least 18 years old** (or the country's minimum age, if higher, such as 21 in the United Arab Emirates) and have full legal capacity. Legal entities may act as Guarantors through a representative with authority to do so.

## 6. Guarantor — liability

6.1. Upon accepting, the Guarantor becomes **jointly and severally liable** with the Renter for payment of the amounts owed in connection with that Booking, **limited to 3 times the total amount of the Booking**, or a lower amount stated in the invitation.

6.2. The Guarantor's liability: (a) relates to a single Booking and its Occurrences; (b) does not extend to other Bookings, nor to non-monetary obligations; (c) is triggered only after an unsuccessful attempt to collect through the Security Deposit and the Renter's payment method, in accordance with the collection order in Penalties and Measures; (d) is automatically extinguished when the Booking is completed with no Incident opened within the reporting deadlines, or upon full payment of the amounts decided.

6.3. The Guarantor is notified of the opening of an Incident that may trigger their liability and may submit a response within the same 48-hour period granted to the Renter, as well as appeal the decision.

6.4. A Guarantor who makes a payment has a right of reimbursement against the Renter for the full amount paid.

6.5. In Brazil, the guarantee has the nature of a limited conventional suretyship (*fiança convencional limitada* — Civil Code, arts. 818 et seq.), with joint and several liability expressly agreed and waiver of the benefit of order (*benefício de ordem*, art. 828, I and II), without prejudice to the operational collection order in clause 6.2(c), which the Platform undertakes to observe. Where the Guarantor is married, the legal requirement of spousal consent applies, if applicable. In other countries, the equivalent legal concept applies (*guarantee*, *fianza*, *Bürgschaft*, *cautionnement*), with local formalities.

## 7. Guarantor data protection

7.1. The Guarantor's data is processed based on performance of the guarantee agreement and the legitimate interest of the Platform and the Host in securing the Booking, under the LGPD (Brazilian General Data Protection Law) and the equivalent laws indicated in Country Rules.

7.2. The data is used only for: sending the invitation, recording acceptance, identity verification, communication about Incidents, and collection. It is not used for marketing without specific consent.

7.3. The Host sees only the Guarantor's name and the acceptance status. The document and contact details are shared with the Host only when necessary for judicial or extrajudicial collection.

7.4. The data of Guarantors who decline or do not respond is deleted within up to 90 days, except where a legal obligation requires otherwise. The data of Guarantors who accept is retained for the statute-of-limitations period of the guaranteed obligations. The Guarantor exercises their data-subject rights through the Data Protection Officer channel indicated in the Privacy Policy: Robson Ravel de Oliveira — info@wmundi.com.

## 8. Final provisions

8.1. The Security Deposit and the Guarantor may coexist in the same Booking. The existence of guarantees does not limit the Renter's liability, who remains fully liable for the damage caused.

8.2. The Platform is not a guarantor of the Renter's obligations to the Host, unless it offers, in writing and under a specific program, some additional protection.
