# Cancellation and Refund Policy

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. General provisions

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and sets out the consequences of cancelling Bookings by the Renter, by the Host, or due to mitigating circumstances, as well as the timeframes and methods of refund.

1.2. Each Listing adopts one of three cancellation policies — **Flexible**, **Moderate** or **Strict** — displayed on the Listing and in the Booking summary before payment. The policy in effect at the time of the Booking applies.

1.3. Time periods are counted in hours until the **start of each Occurrence**, in the Space Time Zone, from the moment the cancellation is recorded on the Platform.

1.4. The policy percentages apply to the **Base Amount** of each Occurrence. The Cleaning Fee, the Service Fee and the tax on the Service Fee follow the specific rules in clauses 5 and 6.

1.5. The rules in this document do not displace more favorable rights guaranteed by mandatory law to the consumer Renter, which always prevail.

## 2. Order of application

2.1. When processing a cancellation by the Renter, the Platform checks, in this order:
- (a) **grace period** (clause 3) — full refund;
- (b) **right of withdrawal** of the Space's country (clause 4) — full refund;
- (c) **the Listing's cancellation policy**, Occurrence by Occurrence (clause 5).

2.2. Mitigating circumstances (clause 8) and cancellation by the Host (clause 7) have their own rules.

## 3. Grace period

3.1. The Renter may cancel with a **full refund** (Base Amount, Cleaning Fee, Service Fee and tax) within **24 hours after the Booking is confirmed**, provided the Booking was made with **at least 48 hours' notice** before the start of the first Occurrence and no Occurrence has begun.

3.2. The grace period applies to all Renters, consumers or not, in all countries.

## 4. Right of withdrawal

4.1. A Renter who qualifies as a consumer may withdraw from the Booking, with a **full refund** of all amounts paid, within the withdrawal period established for the Space's country, counted from the date of the Booking, **provided no Occurrence has begun**.

4.2. Periods configured on the Platform:

| Brazil | 7 days | CDC, Lei 8.078/1990, art. 49 (Consumer Protection Code) |
| Argentina | 10 days | Ley 24.240, art. 34 |

4.3. On the Platform, the period is computed in calendar days from the time of the Booking. Where local law provides for counting in business days or a longer period (for example, "5 días hábiles" in Colombia, Uruguay and Mexico), the legal count more favorable to the consumer prevails, upon request through the Resolution Center.

4.4. In some legal systems (for example, the European Union and the United Kingdom), the law allows the right of withdrawal to be excluded for services performed on a specified date. As a matter of goodwill, the Platform grants the right within the periods in the table to consumers, always before the start of the first Occurrence.

4.5. The right of withdrawal does not apply to Renters who contract for professional purposes when local law does not treat them as consumers; the grace period and the Listing's policy apply to them instead.

## 5. Cancellation policies by the Renter

5.1. Outside the grace period and the withdrawal period, each future Occurrence is assessed individually according to the notice given between the cancellation and its start:

**Flexible Policy**

| Notice before the start of the Occurrence | Refund of the Base Amount |
|---|---|
| 24 hours or more | 100% |
| Less than 24 hours | 50% |

**Moderate Policy**

| Notice before the start of the Occurrence | Refund of the Base Amount |
|---|---|
| 72 hours or more | 100% |
| From 24 hours to less than 72 hours | 50% |
| Less than 24 hours | 0% |

**Strict Policy**

| Notice before the start of the Occurrence | Refund of the Base Amount |
|---|---|
| 168 hours (7 days) or more | 100% |
| From 72 hours to less than 168 hours | 50% |
| Less than 72 hours | 0% |

5.2. **Refund per Occurrence in series.** In Bookings with several Occurrences (consecutive days or weekly recurrence), the Base Amount is divided equally among the Occurrences and each future Occurrence receives the percentage corresponding to its own notice period. Occurrences already begun or completed are not refunded.

5.3. **Cleaning Fee.** The Cleaning Fee of every cancelled Occurrence that has not yet begun is **always refunded in full**, regardless of the policy, since the cleaning service will not be performed.

5.4. **Service Fee and tax.** The Renter's Service Fee and the tax on it are refunded **in proportion to the number of Occurrences that receive a full refund (100%)** of the Base Amount, relative to the total number of Occurrences in the Booking. Occurrences refunded at 50% or 0% do not generate a refund of the corresponding Service Fee.

5.5. **Example.** A weekly series of 4 Occurrences, Moderate policy, cancelled when 30 hours remain before the first one. The first Occurrence receives 50% of its proportional Base Amount; the following three (more than 72 hours' notice) receive 100%. The Cleaning Fee for all 4 Occurrences is refunded in full. The Service Fee and the tax are refunded at 3/4.

5.6. **No-show.** A no-show without cancellation does not generate a refund of the Base Amount or of the Service Fee for the Occurrence. The unused Cleaning Fee is refunded when the Host confirms that cleaning was not necessary.

5.7. **Early departure.** Ending the Occurrence before the scheduled time does not generate a refund for the unused hours.

## 7. Cancellation by the Host

7.1. The Host must honor confirmed Bookings. If the Host cancels, the Renter receives a **100% refund** of all amounts paid for the cancelled Occurrences, and the Platform will use reasonable efforts to suggest similar Spaces.

7.2. In addition, the Host becomes subject to a penalty, calculated on the Base Amount of the cancelled Occurrences and deducted from future payouts or charged through the registered payment method, and to a Warning:

| Notice before the start of the Occurrence | Penalty to the Host | Warning |
|---|---|---|
| 168 hours (7 days) or more | 0% | Yes |
| From 48 hours to less than 168 hours | 10% | Yes |
| Less than 48 hours | 25% | Yes |

7.3. **3 cancellations by the Host in 12 months** result in **suspension** of the Host account, under the terms of Penalties and Measures. The Host may also have the cancelled dates blocked on the calendar and lose search prominence.

7.4. No penalty or Warning applies when the cancellation results from a proven mitigating circumstance (clause 8) or from a serious violation by the Renter duly reported.

## 8. Mitigating circumstances and force majeure

8.1. Mitigating circumstances are considered to be unforeseeable or unavoidable events, arising after the Booking, that prevent it from taking place, such as: natural disasters; closure of the property or building by an authority; a prolonged lack of power or water at the Space; epidemics with official restrictions; death or sudden serious illness of the Renter, the Host, or a close family member; judicial, electoral or military summons; and serious damage to the Space that renders it unusable.

8.2. Once the circumstance is proven with suitable documents (medical certificate, official act, police report, expert report), submitted through the Resolution Center within 14 days of the cancellation, the Renter receives a **full refund** for the affected Occurrences and the Host suffers no penalty or Warning.

8.3. The following are not mitigating circumstances: a change of schedule, lack of End Clients, ordinary weather conditions, foreseeable transportation problems, or obligations known at the time of the Booking.

## 9. Refund timeframes and method

9.1. Refunds are processed by the Platform immediately after the decision and returned through the same payment method, when possible:

| Payment method | Estimated timeframe |
|---|---|
| Credit or debit card, digital wallets (Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, Alipay, WeChat Pay, etc.) | 5 to 10 business days, depending on the issuer; may appear on the following statement |
| Pix, bank transfer, SPEI, SEPA, ACH, Interac, UPI, PayID and similar | Up to 5 business days |
| Boleto, OXXO, konbini, Multibanco and other methods without an identifiable source account | Transfer to the bank account provided by the Renter, within up to 5 business days after it is provided |
| Authorization not yet captured (Request-based Booking or Security Deposit) | Immediate release by the Platform; the release timeframe depends on the issuer |

9.2. Refunds in foreign currency may be subject to exchange-rate variation applied by the issuer, over which the Operator has no control.

9.3. **Optional credit.** Instead of a refund, the Renter may choose to receive Platform credit in the same amount, usable within 12 months. Credit is never imposed; the option of a cash refund is always guaranteed.

## 10. Final provisions

10.1. Amounts owed by the Renter due to Incidents (Penalties and Measures) may be offset against pending refunds, upon detailed notice.

10.2. Questions or disagreements about the refund calculation may be raised through the Resolution and Disputes Center.
