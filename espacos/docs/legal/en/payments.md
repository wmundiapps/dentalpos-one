# International Payments

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and governs the charging, processing, payout, refunds and holds of amounts relating to Bookings.

1.2. Payments are processed by partner payment institutions and acquirers, duly authorized in each jurisdiction. The Operator acts as the Host's limited collection agent: payment made by the Renter to the Platform discharges the Renter's obligation to the Host.

## 2. Currency

2.1. Every Space is priced in the **official currency of the country where it is located** (for example, BRL in Brazil, EUR in Portugal, USD in the United States, Ecuador, Panama and El Salvador). The Booking is charged in that currency.

2.2. The Platform may display approximate amounts in the Renter's currency for reference only. If the Renter's payment method is in another currency, **conversion is performed by the issuer** of the card or account, with a rate, fees and taxes (such as IOF in Brazil) set by the issuer, over which the Operator has no control.

2.3. Amounts are rounded according to the currency's decimal places (for example, zero decimal places for JPY and CLP).

## 3. Price composition

3.1. The total paid by the Renter is composed of:

| Component | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Base Amount | Sum, per Occurrence, of hours × hourly price, or the daily rate for Occurrences of 6 hours or more when more favorable |
| Cleaning Fee | Fixed amount per Occurrence, set by the Host, capped at 30% of the Base Amount |
| Renter's Service Fee | **15%** on (Base Amount + Cleaning Fee) |
| Tax on the Service Fee | Reference rate of the country (e.g.: ISS 5% in Brazil, VAT 16% in Mexico, VAT 23% in Portugal) applied to the Service Fee |
| **Total** | Sum of the items above |

3.2. The payout to the Host is:

| Component | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Base Amount + Cleaning Fee | In full |
| (–) Host's Service Fee | **5%** on (Base Amount + Cleaning Fee) |
| (–) Legal withholdings, penalties and adjustments | Where applicable |
| **Payout** | Result |

3.3. **Example (Brazil).** A 4-hour Booking at R$80/hour, Cleaning Fee of R$30. Base Amount: R$320. Renter's Service Fee: 15% × R$350 = R$52.50. Reference ISS (5%) on the Service Fee: R$2.63. Total paid: R$405.13. Host's Fee: 5% × R$350 = R$17.50. Payout to the Host: R$332.50.

3.4. The full breakdown is displayed before confirmation. There are no hidden charges.

## 5. Authorization, capture and pre-authorization

5.1. **Request-based Booking.** The amount is **authorized** (held) and is only **captured** when the Host accepts. A decline or expiration within 24 hours releases the authorization.

5.2. **Booking with Guarantor.** The amount remains authorized until the Guarantor accepts (48-hour period).

5.3. **Instant Booking.** Capture occurs at confirmation.

5.4. **Security Deposit.** An additional pre-authorization, capped at 3 times the Base Amount, released 72 hours after check-out if there is no Incident.

5.5. **Recurring Bookings.** For series, the total amount may be charged at confirmation or, when local regulation requires it or the Renter chooses, in installments per Occurrence, subject to local rules on recurring payments (for example, RBI *e-mandates* in India).

## 6. Payout to the Host

6.1. The payout is released **24 hours after the start of the first Occurrence** of the Booking, to the account registered by the Host, less the Host's Service Fee and any withholdings. For series, the Platform may release the payout per Occurrence, 24 hours after the start of each one.

6.2. Crediting the Host's account depends on the timeframes of the transfer method and the destination bank. International payouts may be subject to bank fees and currency conversion.

6.3. The Host must maintain a bank account in their own name (or in the name of the legal entity holding the Listing) and up-to-date tax details.

## 7. Holds

7.1. The Platform may withhold, in whole or in part, payouts to the Host when: (a) there is an Incident opened by the Renter or a pending dispute; (b) there are indications of fraud, money laundering, a compromised account, or a serious violation; (c) identification, tax or banking details are missing; (d) the Host has a debt to the Platform (cancellation penalties, refunds owed); (e) there is a judicial or administrative order; (f) a *chargeback* is in progress.

7.2. The hold is communicated to the Host together with the reason and lasts only as long as necessary.

7.3. **Tax withholdings.** Where the law requires the Platform to withhold taxes on payouts (for example, VAT/income tax in Mexico, TCS under the GST in India), the amounts withheld are remitted and disclosed to the Host with the corresponding proof.

## 8. Refunds

8.1. Refunds follow the Cancellation and Refund Policy and are returned through the same payment method: card within 5 to 10 business days depending on the issuer; Pix and transfers within up to 5 business days; boleto, OXXO, konbini and similar methods via transfer to a bank account provided by the Renter.

8.2. Refunds resulting from a Host failure are deducted from the Host's future payouts or charged to the Host.

## 9. Additional charges

9.1. Late departure, damage, extra cleaning, condominium fines, and penalties decided in an Incident are collected in this order: Security Deposit, registered payment method, and Guarantor (Penalties and Measures). The Renter expressly authorizes these charges upon accepting the Terms and receives the breakdown of each one.

## 10. Chargebacks

10.1. The Renter must first seek the Resolution Center before disputing a charge with the issuer. This does not limit the cardholder's rights.

10.2. In case of a *chargeback*, the Platform will present the issuer with evidence of the Booking (acceptance of the Terms and rules, check-in/check-out, messages, photos). If the *chargeback* is upheld due to a Host failure, the amount will be debited from the Host's payouts. Fraudulent or abusive *chargebacks* subject the Renter to suspension and to being charged the amount, plus fees.

## 11. Prohibition on payment outside the Platform

11.1. All payment relating to Bookings must be made through the Platform. Requesting, offering or accepting off-platform payment (including "discount for paying directly," a deposit to an account, direct Pix, or cash) is a serious breach that leads to exclusion of the accounts involved. Off-platform payments are not covered by any Platform protection.

## 12. Receipts and tax documents

12.1. The Renter receives an electronic receipt for each payment, with a breakdown of the components. The Operator issues the tax document relating to the **Service Fee** in accordance with the country's legislation (in Brazil, an electronic service invoice — NFS-e).

12.2. The tax document relating to the **Base Amount and the Cleaning Fee**, when required, is the Host's responsibility, who must issue it to the Renter in accordance with their legislation. The Platform provides the necessary data.

12.3. Legal-entity Renters must provide their tax identification (for example, CNPJ, RFC, NIF, VAT number) to be included on the documents.

## 13. Fraud prevention, AML and KYC

13.1. The Platform adopts **know your customer (KYC)** and **anti-money laundering and counter-terrorist financing (AML/CFT)** procedures, including identity verification and verification of ownership of bank accounts, screening against sanctions lists, monitoring of atypical transactions, operational limits, and enhanced verification (3-D Secure, link confirmation).

13.2. The Platform may decline, cancel or hold payments and payouts showing indications of fraud, use of a third party's card, simulated transactions, or transactions inconsistent with the profile, and may notify the competent authorities as required by law.

13.3. Card data is tokenized by PCI DSS-certified processors; the Platform does not store the full card number or the security code.
