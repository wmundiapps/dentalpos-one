# Resolution and Disputes Center

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose and principles

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and governs the Resolution Center, the Platform's official channel for recording Incidents, negotiating, mediating and deciding disagreements between Hosts, Renters and Guarantors relating to Bookings, as well as appeals against the Platform's decisions.

1.2. The Resolution Center observes the principles of impartiality, the right to be heard, a full defense, speediness, reasoned decisions, and proportionality.

1.3. Use of the Resolution Center is free of charge and **does not prevent** the User from resorting to official consumer protection bodies or to the judiciary at any time.

## 2. How to open an Incident

2.1. The Incident is opened on the Booking's page, under the "Report a problem" option, indicating:
- (a) the type of Incident (for example: late departure, damage, extra cleaning, rule violation, exceeding capacity, unauthorized activity, subletting, smoking or substances, condominium fine, harassment, payment outside the Platform, no-show, inaccurate listing, lack of access, safety);
- (b) an objective description of the facts;
- (c) the evidence (clause 3);
- (d) the amount requested, if any, with the corresponding proof.

2.2. **Deadlines for opening**, counted from the end of the Occurrence:

| Type | Deadline |
|---|---|
| Late departure, extra cleaning, smoking/substances, no-show, inaccurate listing, lack of access | 24 hours |
| Damage, rule violation, exceeding capacity, unauthorized activity, safety | 72 hours |
| Subletting | 168 hours (7 days) |
| Condominium fine, harassment or discrimination, payment outside the Platform | 720 hours (30 days) |

2.3. **Urgent situations.** In case of risk to the safety of persons, contact local emergency services first, and then the Platform. Access problems or issues with the condition of the Space at the start of the Occurrence must be reported immediately, to allow a real-time solution.

2.4. Questions about refunds, charges and cancellation that do not involve the other party's conduct may be submitted directly to customer support, through the same channel.

## 3. Evidence

3.1. The following evidence is admissible:
- (a) **time-stamped photos and videos**, especially those recorded at **check-in and check-out** through the Platform;
- (b) Listing photos and prior maintenance records;
- (c) messages exchanged through the Platform;
- (d) access records (reception/security, electronic lock, common-area cameras);
- (e) quotes, invoices and receipts for repair or cleaning;
- (f) notice of a condominium fine;
- (g) police reports, expert reports, medical certificates, and official documents.

3.2. Evidence may not contain health data or data identifying End Clients; where unavoidable, it must be anonymized or redacted.

3.3. Messages exchanged outside the Platform have reduced evidentiary value and do not replace the Platform's records.

## 4. Response and negotiation

4.1. The party against whom the Incident was opened (and the Guarantor, when they may be held liable) is notified and has **48 hours** to: (a) accept; (b) contest, with their own evidence; or (c) submit a counterproposal.

4.2. During that period, the parties may negotiate through the Resolution Center. A recorded agreement closes the Incident and is carried out by the Platform.

4.3. If there is no response within 48 hours, the Incident proceeds to review based on the available evidence.

## 5. Mediation and decision by the Platform

5.1. If there is a dispute or no agreement, the Platform's team acts as a **mediator**, and may request additional information from the parties, with a reasonable deadline to respond.

5.2. If there is no agreement, the Platform **decides** on a reasoned basis, based on the Terms, the Booking and Use Rules, Penalties and Measures, the Listing's rules, and the evidence, and may: reject the request; grant the amount in whole or in part; apply or not apply a Warning; order a refund; or apply suspension or exclusion measures.

5.3. The decision states the amount, the source of the charge (Security Deposit, payment method, or Guarantor), any Warning, and the reasoning. The Platform uses reasonable efforts to decide within 10 business days after the end of the response period.

5.4. The Platform's decision is administrative and contractual: it binds the parties within the Platform, but **does not prevent judicial review** of the merits.

## 6. Appeal

6.1. Any party may file an **appeal within 7 days** of notice of the decision, through the Resolution Center itself, presenting the grounds and any new evidence.

6.2. The appeal is reviewed by a person other than the one who issued the original decision. The charge decided may be suspended during the appeal, except in cases of a serious breach or risk to safety.

6.3. If the appeal is granted, the Platform refunds the amounts charged and removes any improper Warnings. The decision on the appeal closes the administrative instance within the Platform.

6.4. Account suspensions and exclusions may also be appealed within the same period. In the European Union, the User also has access to the internal complaint-handling and out-of-court dispute settlement mechanisms provided for in the Digital Services Act (DSA).

## 8. Online dispute resolution (ODR)

8.1. Consumers in the European Union may resort to the alternative dispute resolution (ADR) bodies notified in their Member State, in accordance with the applicable European consumer dispute resolution regime. In Brazil, the public platform **consumidor.gov.br** is the preferred channel for online resolution. The Operator undertakes to participate in good faith in these procedures when registered or when required by law.

## 9. External arbitration and mediation

9.1. Outside of consumer relationships, the parties may, by mutual agreement and through a specific instrument, submit the dispute to mediation or arbitration, before a chosen chamber, where permitted by applicable law.

9.2. **Arbitration is never imposed on the consumer.** In Brazil, an arbitration clause in an adhesion contract is effective only if the adhering party takes the initiative to institute arbitration or expressly agrees to it, in an attached document or in bold, with a signature or special initialing (Lei 9.307/1996, art. 4, § 2), and a clause requiring the compulsory use of arbitration in consumer relationships is void (CDC, art. 51, VII).

## 10. The Judiciary

10.1. Nothing in this document prevents access to the judiciary. The consumer User may bring an action in the courts of their domicile, as provided in the Terms of Use.
