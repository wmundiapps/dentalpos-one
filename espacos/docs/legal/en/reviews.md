# Reviews Policy

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and governs reviews published on the Platform, which exist to build trust between Hosts and Renters, based on real experiences.

1.2. There are three types of review:
- (a) **Renter reviews the Space and the Host**;
- (b) **Host reviews the Renter**;
- (c) **End Client reviews the Space** — an **optional** tool, activated only at the Renter's request.

## 2. Who may review and deadline

2.1. Only the parties to a completed Booking (with check-out or the end of the last Occurrence) may review one another, once per Booking.

2.2. The deadline to submit a review is **14 days** after the end of the Booking.

2.3. Each review contains an overall rating of 1 to 5 stars, ratings of 1 to 5 per criterion, and a public comment of **10 to 2,000 characters**. It may also contain a private note to the other party, which is not published.

## 3. Criteria

3.1. **Renter reviews the Space and the Host:**

| Criterion | What is assessed |
|---|---|
| Cleanliness | Hygiene condition of the Space upon arrival |
| Accuracy | Match between the Listing and reality |
| Equipment | Presence, function and condition of the advertised equipment |
| Location | Access, safety and convenience of the address |
| Communication | Clarity and responsiveness of the Host |
| Value for money | Suitability of the price for the experience |

3.2. **Host reviews the Renter:**

| Criterion | What is assessed |
|---|---|
| Punctuality | Arrival and departure at the booked times |
| Care | Upkeep and cleanliness of the Space and equipment |
| Rules | Compliance with the Space's, building's and Platform's rules |
| Communication | Clarity and courtesy |
| Would recommend? | "Yes/no" indication of whether they would recommend the Renter to other Hosts |

## 4. Double-blind review

4.1. Reviews between Host and Renter are **double-blind**: neither party sees the other's review before submitting their own. Both become visible once both parties have reviewed or when the 14-day deadline ends, whichever occurs first.

4.2. Once published, a review cannot be edited by its author, except to correct a material error upon request to the Platform.

## 5. Public response

5.1. The person reviewed may publish **one public response** to each review received, subject to the same content rules. The response must be objective and may not contain personal attacks or third parties' personal data.

## 6. End Client reviews (optional)

6.1. **Activation.** The Renter may, **if they wish**, activate collection of reviews from their End Clients about the Space for a Booking. The Host may not require this activation.

6.2. **Invitations.** The Renter generates **single-use links**, each valid for **14 days**, up to a limit of **50 invitations per Booking**, and delivers them to End Clients through the channel of their choice. The Platform does not receive End Clients' contact details for this purpose.

6.3. **Anonymity.** End Client reviews are **anonymous**: they require no registration, display no name or identification, and the author may provide only an optional nickname.

6.4. **Criteria.** The End Client reviews exclusively the **Space**, on the criteria: **comfort, cleanliness, accessibility and location**. The professional (Renter), the service provided, or the treatment received is not reviewed.

6.5. **Prohibition on health data.** It is **prohibited** to include in End Client reviews **health data, diagnoses, treatments, procedures**, names, identification documents, contact details, or any information that could identify patients, clients, or the reason for the appointment. The form displays this notice, and reviews that violate this rule are removed.

6.6. **Professional confidentiality.** The Renter must assess, in light of their professional code of ethics, whether delivering the invitation is compatible with the confidentiality of the appointment (for example, in psychology), and is solely responsible for that decision. Delivery of the link may not be conditioned on a benefit or done in a way that exposes the patient.

6.7. **Display.** End Client reviews are displayed separately, identified as such, and form their own indicators for the Space.

## 7. Content rules

7.1. Reviews must be honest, based on a real experience, and relevant to the Booking. Content is prohibited that:
- (a) contains third parties' personal data, health data, or confidential information;
- (b) is discriminatory, offensive, obscene, threatening, or constitutes harassment;
- (c) contains hate speech or incitement to violence;
- (d) contains advertising, external links, or contact details;
- (e) refers to matters unrelated to the Booking or to uninvolved third parties;
- (f) is deliberately false or misleading.

## 8. Fake reviews, purchased reviews and extortion

8.1. It is prohibited to: (a) submit a review without having taken part in the Booking; (b) offer or receive payment, a discount, a refund, or a benefit in exchange for a review or its removal; (c) create simulated Bookings to generate reviews; (d) use multiple accounts; (e) **threaten to publish a negative review to obtain an advantage** (extortion) or retaliate with a negative review against someone who opened a legitimate Incident; (f) generate End Client invitations and fill them out personally or through persons who were not served.

8.2. Such conduct is a serious violation, subjecting the offender to a Warning and, depending on the case, exclusion, in addition to removal of the reviews involved.

## 9. Moderation and removal

9.1. The Platform does not edit the content of reviews, but may **remove**, in whole or in part, reviews that violate this document, the law, or a court decision, or hide reviews linked to a Booking cancelled for fraud.

9.2. Any User may report a review through the Platform. Review is carried out within a reasonable timeframe, with notice to the author in case of removal, who may appeal.

9.3. The Platform does not remove reviews merely because they are negative. Disagreements should be addressed through a public response.

9.4. Reviews related to a Booking subject to an Incident may be kept hidden until the decision, to prevent retaliation.

## 10. Use of reviews

10.1. Average ratings are calculated from published reviews and displayed on the profile and the Listing. Reviews may be taken into account in ranking search results and in recognition programs.

10.2. In compliance with transparency rules (such as the European Union's Digital Services Act and consumer protection laws), the Platform discloses that only parties to Bookings that were actually completed, and End Clients with a valid invitation link, may submit reviews.
