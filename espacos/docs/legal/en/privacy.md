# Privacy Policy

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Who we are and who this applies to

1.1. This Policy explains how **Instituto Ravel de Ensino Superior Ltda.**, CNPJ **03.162.275/0001-10**, headquartered at **[ENDEREÇO]** (the "Operator"), processes personal data on the SpaceHour Platform, acting as **data controller**.

1.2. It applies to Hosts, Renters, Guarantors, End Clients who use the review tool, website visitors, and representatives of legal entities.

1.3. **Data Protection Officer (DPO):** **Robson Ravel de Oliveira** — contact: **info@wmundi.com**. Where required by local law, the Operator will appoint a representative in the country or in the European Union (GDPR art. 27) and a grievance officer in India.

1.4. **Roles.** With respect to the data of their own patients, clients and students, the **Renter** and the **Host** are independent data controllers, responsible for the processing they carry out in their professional activity. The Platform does not receive medical/clinical records and must not be used for that purpose.

## 4. Sharing

4.1. **Between Host and Renter.** After a Booking is confirmed, the Host receives the Renter's name, public profile, declared activity, number of people, and, when the Listing requires it, the indication of verified professional registration; the Renter receives the Space's full address and access instructions. Phone number and email are not displayed, and the parties communicate through the Platform.

4.2. **Guarantor.** The Guarantor receives the Booking data necessary for their decision (Renter's name, Space, dates, total, and limit). The Host sees only the Guarantor's name and status; other data is shared only if necessary for collection.

4.3. **Payment processors** and financial institutions, for authorization, capture, Security Deposit, refunds, payouts, and fraud prevention.

4.4. **Service providers** (processors) under contract: cloud hosting, sending of emails and notifications, identity verification, customer support, data analysis, which process data only in accordance with our instructions.

4.5. **Public, judicial and tax authorities and professional boards**, when required by law, an order, or for the exercise of rights.

4.6. **Corporate transactions**, in case of a merger, acquisition or reorganization, with the safeguards of this Policy maintained.

4.7. We do not sell personal data.

## 5. International transfers

5.1. The Platform operates in several countries and may transfer data outside the data subject's country, including to cloud providers, and between the Renter's country and the Space's country.

5.2. Transfers comply with the mechanisms required by applicable law, such as: standard contractual clauses approved by the ANPD (Brazil, Resolução CD/ANPD No. 19/2024); the European Commission's standard contractual clauses and adequacy decisions (GDPR, arts. 45 and 46); the UK's IDTA/Addendum; PIPL (China) mechanisms, including local storage and security assessment when required; and the equivalent rules of the other laws indicated in Country Rules.

## 6. Retention

| Data | Period |
|---|---|
| Active account | For as long as the account exists |
| Bookings, payments and tax documents | The tax and accounting legal period (in Brazil, generally 5 years; in other countries, the local period) |
| Application access logs | A minimum of 6 months (Marco Civil da Internet), or the local period |
| Incidents, evidence and Warnings | Up to 5 years after the Booking or the applicable statute-of-limitations period |

| Data of accounts excluded for a serious violation | The minimum necessary to prevent re-registration, for the legally permitted period |

6.1. Once these periods end, the data is deleted or anonymized.

## 7. Data subject rights

7.1. The data subject may, under applicable law: confirm the existence of processing; access their data; correct incomplete or inaccurate data; request anonymization, blocking, or deletion of unnecessary data; request portability; obtain information about sharing; withdraw consent; object to processing based on legitimate interest; request review of automated decisions; and file a complaint with the data protection authority (in Brazil, the ANPD; in the European Union, the supervisory authority of the Member State; in the United Kingdom, the ICO; among others).

7.2. Requests must be sent to the Data Protection Officer at **info@wmundi.com** or through the account's privacy section. We respond within the legal deadline (for example, 15 days under the LGPD for the full statement; 1 month under the GDPR, extendable). We may request identity confirmation.

7.3. Some data may be retained even after a deletion request, when necessary for compliance with a legal obligation, the exercise of rights in proceedings, or fraud prevention.

7.4. California residents (CCPA/CPRA) additionally have the right to know, delete, correct, and opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of data; the Operator does not sell or share data for cross-context behavioral advertising without consent.

## 8. Cookies

8.1. We use cookies and similar technologies: (a) **essential** cookies, for login, security, language and Booking functionality, which do not require consent; (b) **preference** cookies; (c) **analytics** cookies; and (d) **marketing** cookies, the latter three only with consent where required by law, manageable at any time through the cookie panel.

## 9. End Client data in reviews

9.1. The End Client review tool is optional, activated by the Renter. The End Client accesses it via a single-use link, without registration, and provides only ratings about the Space (comfort, cleanliness, accessibility, location), a comment, and an optional nickname.

9.2. The Platform **does not collect** the End Client's name, contact details, or health data. The form warns against including data that could identify the patient or the appointment; content containing such data is removed. Minimal technical data (such as IP address) is processed only for abuse prevention and retained for the access-log period.

## 10. Minors

10.1. The Platform is not intended for persons below the minimum age set in each country (18 years; 21 years in the United Arab Emirates). We do not knowingly collect data from minors as Users. Accounts identified as belonging to minors are closed.

10.2. Minor End Clients should not fill out reviews without the participation of their legal guardian.

## 11. Security

11.1. We adopt technical and administrative measures appropriate to the risk, including: encryption in transit (TLS); password storage as a hash; tokenization of card data by PCI DSS-certified processors (the Platform does not store the full card number); role-based access control; audit logs; disclosure of the Space's full address only after confirmation; and staff training.

11.2. In case of a security incident that may cause a relevant risk or harm, we will notify the competent authority and the data subjects within the legal deadlines (for example, in accordance with the ANPD's regulation in Brazil and within 72 hours to the authority under the GDPR).
