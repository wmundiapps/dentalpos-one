# Booking and Use Rules

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose and scope

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and governs how Bookings are made, confirmed and used on the Platform. Capitalized terms have the meaning given to them in the Terms of Use.

1.2. The Platform exists to enable **shared use, by the hour, shift or day, of ready-to-use Spaces during their idle time slots**. It is not a means of contracting continuous occupancy, long-term leasing, a business domicile or housing.

1.3. The limits set out in this document are applied automatically by the system at the time of Booking and **may not be set aside by agreement between Host and Renter**. They exist to: (a) preserve the nature of a license for the temporary use of an equipped space, avoiding characterization as a lease and the acquisition of commercial-point (*ponto comercial*) status or a right to renewal (in Brazil, Law No. 8,245/1991, and equivalent rules in other countries); (b) ensure turnover and access to the Spaces for various professionals; (c) protect the Host against claims of possession or permanent occupation.

## 2. Time and duration limits

2.1. The following limits apply to every Booking:

| Parameter | Rule |
|---|---|
| Time granularity | Start and end in multiples of **30 minutes** (e.g., 8:00 a.m., 8:30 a.m., 9:00 a.m.) |
| Minimum duration per Occurrence | **1 hour**, or the higher minimum set by the Host in the Listing |
| Maximum duration per Occurrence | **12 hours** |
| Permitted time window | Between **6:00 a.m.** and **11:00 p.m.** in the Space Time Zone |
| Overnight stay | **Prohibited** — each Occurrence begins and ends on the same day |
| Consecutive days | A maximum of **5 consecutive days** in a single Booking |
| Weekly recurrence | A maximum of **12 weeks** per series (same day of the week) |
| Occurrences per Booking | A maximum of **12** |
| Maximum advance booking | **180 days** counted from the current date in the Space Time Zone |
| Minimum advance booking | **1 hour** before the start of the Occurrence |
| Hour cap | **120 hours** per Renter, per Space, within any rolling **30-day** window |
| Active recurring series | A maximum of **2** simultaneous series per Renter for the same Space |
| Gap after a maximum series | **14 days** after the end of a 12-week series before starting a new series for the same Space |

2.2. **Permitted patterns.** A Booking with more than one Occurrence must follow exactly one of these patterns: (a) **consecutive days** (successive dates, up to 5); or (b) **weekly recurrence** (same day of the week, with a 7-day interval, up to 12 weeks). Duplicate dates or irregular combinations are not accepted; in that case the Renter must make separate Bookings, which remain subject to the hour cap and the other limits.

2.3. **Availability window.** Each Occurrence must fit entirely within one of the idle-time windows the Host has provided for that day of the week, and may not fall on a date blocked by the Host.

2.4. **Daily rate.** If the Host offers a daily rate (full shift), it is automatically applied to Occurrences of 6 hours or more whenever it is more favorable to the Renter than the hourly rate.

2.5. **Attempts to circumvent the limits.** It is prohibited to split Bookings, use third-party accounts, create multiple accounts, or combine Bookings across Spaces of the same Host for the purpose of obtaining continuous occupancy. Such conduct constitutes a rule violation and may amount to subletting or misuse, with the consequences set out in Penalties and Measures.

## 3. Expressly prohibited uses

3.1. Under no circumstances may the Renter:
- (a) use the Space's address as a **headquarters, branch, tax domicile, mailing address**, address on a professional or health registration, commercial address, or in advertising material as though it were their own establishment;
- (b) affix a **sign, banner or permanent identification** at the Space or building (temporary identification during the Occurrence, removed at the end, is permitted if authorized by the Host);
- (c) **stay overnight**, reside, sleep, or use the Space as housing, even temporarily;
- (d) **store belongings, materials, equipment, records or stock between Bookings**, except for a storage service expressly offered by the Host in the Listing and under its conditions;
- (e) **sublet, assign, lend**, or allow an undeclared third party to use the Space;
- (f) carry out an activity different from the one declared, an activity prohibited by the Host, or an activity for which they lack the required legal qualification;
- (g) receive mail, deliveries or visitors outside the Occurrence's time window;
- (h) carry out construction work, alterations, painting, installations or layout changes.

3.2. Violation of this clause subjects the Renter to the penalties for rule violation, unauthorized activity, or subletting, as applicable, in addition to cancellation of future Occurrences without refund where permitted by law.

## 4. Types of Booking

4.1. **Instant Booking.** If the Listing is configured for instant booking, the Booking is confirmed immediately after payment authorization and, where required, after the Guarantor's acceptance.

4.2. **Request-based Booking.** In other cases, the Booking has the status "awaiting host" and the amount is only authorized (not captured). The Host has **24 hours** to accept or decline. If there is no response within that period, the request **automatically expires** and the authorization is released in full. Declines may not be based on discriminatory criteria.

4.3. **Booking with Guarantor.** When the Listing requires a Guarantor, the Booking has the status "awaiting guarantor" until accepted by the Guarantor, who has **48 hours** to respond (Guarantor and Security Deposit document). A decline or silence by the Guarantor ends the request at no cost.

4.4. **Booking statuses.** A Booking may be: awaiting guarantor; awaiting host; confirmed; in use (checked in); completed; cancelled by the Renter; cancelled by the Host; declined; expired; or no-show.

## 5. Cleaning buffer

5.1. The Host may set a preparation and cleaning interval, in minutes, which is automatically blocked before and after each Occurrence. No other Booking may overlap with this interval.

5.2. The buffer is not charged to the Renter and **does not authorize** the Renter to arrive before the start time or leave after the end time. Staying beyond the scheduled time constitutes a late departure (Penalties and Measures).

## 6. Time zone

6.1. All Booking, cancellation, check-in, check-out times and deadlines tied to the start of an Occurrence are expressed and calculated in the **Space Time Zone** (for example, America/Sao_Paulo, America/Manaus, Europe/Lisbon, Asia/Tokyo), including during daylight-saving transitions.

6.2. The Platform displays the time zone alongside the times. A Renter located in a different time zone is responsible for checking the Space's local time.

## 7. Declaration of activity and number of people

7.1. In every Booking, the Renter declares: (a) the **activity** they will carry out (for example, "clinical dental care, no surgical procedures", "individual psychotherapy sessions", "class for 20 students"); and (b) the **number of people** who will be at the Space simultaneously, including themselves, assistants and End Clients.

7.2. The number of people may not exceed the capacity stated in the Listing. The activity must be compatible with the Space's category, with the activities permitted and prohibited by the Host, with the building's rules, and with applicable law.

7.3. The Host may decline requests or open an Incident when the activity actually carried out differs from the one declared.

## 8. Professional registration

8.1. When the Host marks the Listing as **"requires professional registration"**, only Renters with professional registration **verified** by the Platform may complete the Booking. The competent body varies by category and country (for example, CRO, CRM, CRP, OAB, CREFITO, CRN or CRMV in Brazil; GDC, GMC or HCPC in the United Kingdom; cédula profesional/SEP in Mexico; Ahpra in Australia), as set out in the Country Rules document.

8.2. The Renter represents that the registration is active, not suspended, and covers the declared activity. Any suspension, revocation or restriction must be reported immediately, and use of Spaces that require the registration is prohibited for as long as it persists.

8.3. Even when the Listing does not require registration, the Renter is solely responsible for holding the legal qualifications necessary for the activity they carry out.

## 9. Personal use of the Space

9.1. The Booking is personal. The Space may only be used by the Renter holding the Booking and by the persons they have declared (assistants, End Clients, students, participants), within the declared number. The Renter is liable for the acts of all persons who enter the Space under their Booking.

9.2. When the Renter is a legal entity, it must designate the responsible professional who will be present, who must meet the Listing's professional registration requirements.

## 10. Check-in and check-out

10.1. **Access.** The full address and access instructions are revealed only after the Booking is confirmed.

10.2. **Check-in.** Upon arrival, the Renter checks in on the Platform, and it is recommended that they record time-stamped photos of the condition of the Space and equipment. Any irregularities (dirtiness, damage, non-working equipment) must be reported before use begins, preferably within 15 minutes, so as not to be attributed to the Renter.

10.3. **Check-out.** At the end, the Renter must leave the Space in the condition in which it was received, turn off equipment, close windows and doors as instructed, return keys or cards, and check out, preferably with photos.

10.4. **Punctuality.** The Renter may not enter before the start time or remain after the end time. There is a 10-minute grace period after the end time; beyond that, the late-departure charge set out in Penalties and Measures applies.

10.5. **No-show.** If the Renter neither shows up nor cancels, the Occurrence is marked as a no-show and **there is no refund** of the Base Amount for that Occurrence.

10.6. **Lack of access.** If the Renter is unable to access the Space due to the Host's fault, they must notify the Host and open a "lack of access" Incident within 24 hours; once the fact is proven, they receive a full refund for the affected Occurrence.

## 11. Booking changes

11.1. Changes to date, time or number of people may be requested through the Platform and are subject to the Host's acceptance, availability, and compliance with all the limits in this document. Any price difference is charged or refunded. A reduction in hours is treated as a partial cancellation, subject to the cancellation policy.
