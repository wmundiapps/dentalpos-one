# Penalties and Measures

Version 2026-09-24 · Effective from 2026-09-24

## 1. Purpose and principles

1.1. This document forms part of the Terms of Use and establishes the monetary penalties, Warnings and other measures applicable to Renters and Hosts who breach the Terms, the Booking and Use Rules, the Conduct and Respect for Spaces Rules, or a Listing's internal rules.

1.2. Penalties observe the principles of **proportionality, reasonableness, and the right to be heard and to a full defense**. No charge is made without an Incident record, evidence, and an opportunity for the accused party to respond, except for the automatic charge for a late departure recorded at check-out, which may also be contested.

1.3. Penalties have the nature of a compensatory penalty clause and a reasonable pre-estimate of damages. They do not exclude compensation for damages proven to be greater where the law permits, and are not stacked so as to punish the same act twice.

## 2. Table of penalties applicable to the Renter

2.1. Amounts are calculated in relation to the Booking in which the event occurred. "Base Amount" is the sum of the hourly or daily prices of the Booking's Occurrences, excluding fees.

| Violation | Reference penalty | Warning | Deadline for the Host to report |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Late departure** (*overstay*) | 10-minute grace period; thereafter, 15-minute blocks at 1.5x the hourly rate; if the delay exceeds 30 minutes, all blocks at 2x the hourly rate (clause 3), plus compensation to the next affected Renter | No (repeated occurrence may generate a Warning for rule violation) | 24 hours |
| **Extra cleaning** | Proven cost of the additional cleaning | No | 24 hours |
| **Damage** to the Space, furniture or equipment | Proven cost of repair or replacement (clause 4) | No, except for intentional damage or damage due to gross negligence | 72 hours |
| **Violation of rules** of the Space, the building or the Platform | 20% of the Base Amount | Yes | 72 hours |
| **Exceeding capacity** (more people than declared or than the capacity) | 25% of the Base Amount | Yes | 72 hours |
| **Unauthorized activity** (different from the one declared, prohibited by the Host, or without qualification) | 50% of the Base Amount | Yes | 72 hours |
| **Subletting** or assignment to a third party | 100% of the Base Amount; possible account exclusion | Yes — serious breach | 168 hours (7 days) |
| **Smoking or use of substances** prohibited at the Space | Amount equivalent to 2 hours of the Space's hourly price, plus the cost of specialized cleaning, if any | Yes | 24 hours |
| **Condominium/building fine** caused by the Renter | Pass-through of the amount of the fine actually applied, upon copy of the notice | Yes | 720 hours (30 days) |
| **Harassment or discrimination** | Account exclusion; without prejudice to legal liability | Yes — serious breach | 720 hours (30 days) |
| **Payment outside the Platform** (requesting, offering or accepting) | Exclusion of the accounts of those involved | Yes — serious breach | 720 hours (30 days) |
| **No-show** | Loss of the Occurrence's Base Amount (Cancellation Policy); no additional penalty | No | 24 hours |

2.2. Reporting deadlines are counted from the end of the Occurrence in which the event took place or, for condominium fines, from the end of the Occurrence to which the fine relates. Incidents reported after the deadline may exceptionally be accepted when the fact could not have been known earlier (for example, hidden damage), at the Platform's reasoned discretion.

## 3. Late departure — calculation rule

3.1. The delay is measured from the Occurrence's end time to the actual check-out (Platform record, the Host's access record, or other suitable evidence).

3.2. Calculation:
- (a) delay of up to **10 minutes**: no charge (grace period);
- (b) delay of more than 10 minutes and up to **30 minutes**: the total delay is rounded up in **15-minute** blocks, and each block is charged at **1.5 times** the proportional hourly rate;
- (c) delay of more than **30 minutes**: the total delay is rounded up in 15-minute blocks, and each block is charged at **2 times** the proportional hourly rate.

3.3. Examples, for a Space priced at 100 per hour:

| Delay | 15-minute blocks | Multiplier | Charge |
|---|---|---|---|
| 8 min | — | — | 0 |
| 20 min | 2 | 1.5x | 75 |
| 30 min | 2 | 1.5x | 75 |
| 40 min | 3 | 2x | 150 |
| 60 min | 4 | 2x | 200 |

3.4. If the delay prevents or reduces another Renter's Occurrence, the delayed Renter is also liable for **compensation to the affected Renter**, limited to the amount the Platform refunds to that Renter, in addition to a possible Warning for rule violation in case of repeated occurrence.

## 4. Damage and extra cleaning

4.1. The Renter is liable for damage caused, by act or omission, by themselves, by their assistants, End Clients and visitors, excluding natural wear and tear from normal use.

4.2. The Host must prove: (a) the prior condition (Listing photos, check-in photos, maintenance records); (b) the damage or dirtiness (time-stamped photos or videos); (c) the cost (a quote from at least one independent provider, invoice or receipt). For equipment, the cost of repair is considered or, if not feasible, the market value of equivalent equipment with reasonable depreciation.

4.3. Extra cleaning is owed when the Space requires cleaning beyond the routine level (for example, biological waste not disposed of properly, stains, excess trash).

## 5. Procedure

5.1. **Opening.** The Incident is opened through the Resolution Center, within the reporting deadline, with a description, evidence and the amount requested.

5.2. **Response.** The accused party is notified and has **48 hours** to accept, contest, or present a proposal, with their own evidence. Silence may be interpreted as agreement, but does not exempt the Platform from reviewing the evidence.

5.3. **Decision.** The Platform decides on a reasoned basis, and may reduce, uphold or set aside the penalty, and apply or not apply a Warning. An appeal is available within 7 days under the Resolution and Disputes Center.

5.4. **Order of collection.** Amounts acknowledged or decided are collected in this order:
- (1) the pre-authorized **Security Deposit**, if still in effect;
- (2) the **payment method** registered by the Renter;
- (3) the **Guarantor**, up to the limit of their liability.

5.5. If a debt persists, the Platform may suspend the account until it is settled and adopt the collection measures permitted by law, without listing on default registries without prior notice under the terms of applicable law.

5.6. Amounts charged for damage, extra cleaning, condominium fines, delay, and percentage-based penalties are passed on to the Host (or to the affected Renter, as applicable), less only payment-processing costs, where applicable.

## 6. Warning system

6.1. Warnings are counted within a rolling **12-month (365-day)** window:

| Warnings in the last 12 months | Consequence |
|---|---|
| 1 or 2 | Recorded in the history and email notice |
| **3** | **90-day suspension** (cannot make new Bookings nor publish/receive Bookings, as applicable to the role) |
| **5** | **Permanent exclusion** of the account |

6.2. **Serious breaches** — subletting, harassment or discrimination, payment outside the Platform, and risk to safety — may lead to **immediate exclusion**, regardless of the number of Warnings.

6.3. During suspension, future Bookings already confirmed may be cancelled by the Platform, with a full refund to the innocent counterparty.

6.4. Warnings resulting from a decision overturned on appeal are removed.

## 7. Penalties applicable to the Host

| Violation | Consequence | Warning | Deadline for the Renter to report |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Inaccurate Listing** (Space, equipment, capacity or conditions significantly different from what was advertised) | Refund to the Renter proportional to the harm, up to 100% of the affected Occurrence; obligation to correct the Listing | Yes | 24 hours |
| **Lack of access** (Space closed, no key, occupied by third parties) | 100% refund of the Occurrence to the Renter | Yes | 24 hours |
| **Safety** (risk to physical integrity, irregular facilities, dangerous equipment) | Immediate suspension of the Listing; possible exclusion | Yes — serious breach | 72 hours |
| **Cancellation by the Host** | Penalty of 0%, 10% or 25% of the Base Amount, depending on notice given (Cancellation Policy) | Yes | — |
| Harassment, discrimination or payment outside the Platform | Exclusion | Yes — serious breach | 30 days |

7.1. **3 cancellations by the Host in 12 months** lead to suspension of the Host account, applying the same Warning system as clause 6.

7.2. Refunds owed to the Renter due to a Host failure are deducted from the Host's payouts or charged to the Host.

## 8. Reasonableness limits and local laws

8.1. No penalty will be applied in an amount or manner that applicable law considers abusive or disproportionate. In particular:
- (a) in **Brazil**, the Código de Defesa do Consumidor (Consumer Protection Code, arts. 39 and 51), the Civil Code (art. 413 — equitable reduction of the penalty clause), and the prohibition on manifestly excessive advantage are observed;
- (b) in **Australia**, penalties and cancellation fees must be reasonable under the unfair contract terms rules of the Australian Consumer Law;
- (c) in the **European Union** and the **United Kingdom**, Directive 93/13/EEC and the Consumer Rights Act 2015 on unfair terms are observed;
- (d) in other countries, the consumer and contract laws indicated in Country Rules.

8.2. The Platform may, on its own initiative, reduce penalties when the amount proves excessive in light of the nature and purpose of the Booking, the parties' conduct, and the actual harm.

8.3. The sum of the percentage-based penalties for a single Booking (rule violation, exceeding capacity, unauthorized activity and subletting) will not exceed 100% of the Base Amount, without prejudice to compensation for proven damage and costs.

## 9. Precautionary measures

9.1. In case of indications of a serious breach or a risk to the safety of persons, the Platform may, as a precautionary measure, suspend the account or the Listing, cancel future Bookings, and withhold related payouts, for as long as necessary to investigate, notifying the affected party.

9.2. The Platform may notify the competent authorities, including professional boards, when there are indications of a crime, a serious ethical violation, or a risk to public health, in accordance with data protection legislation.
